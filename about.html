About Me

Hi! My name is Bella Bertagnolli. I'm a Software Engineer at IBM working on the Delivery Intelligence Team, where I build AI/ML risk predictor models and data pipelines to spot risky code changes before they reach production. I graduated from the University of Utah with a B.S. in Computer Science in 2024.

My core technical interests include machine learning, cloud infrastructure, and web development. Outside of engineering, I'm deeply passionate about visual art and music. Problem solving and learning new things are my favorite parts of life, so you'll usually find me diving headfirst into a new creative project or hobby!

Expertise

Machine Learning

I've worked on multiple machine learning projects related to image recognition and generation in professional and personal settings. My most recent project at work is an XGBoost prediction model that is trained on all deployments and incidents of the related services.

Full Stack Development

I've worked on multiple projects across the front end and back end, most recently an app for new parents for creating, tracking, and sharing baby schedules written in Dart and using Google Firestore.

Agile Development

I develop using Agile techniques and have been on multiple cross-functional teams that use the Scrum methodology to build great software. I am comfortable running sprints and retrospectives.

Collaboration and Teamwork

I love collaborating in teams to bounce ideas off of one another and build better products together.
